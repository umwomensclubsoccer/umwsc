Moe Anani

Position
Assistant Coach / Women's Club Soccer

Coach Moe is a valued member of the UMWSC coaching staff. He brings a deep understanding of the game and leadership experience as a former captain of the University of Michigan-Dearborn Men’s Soccer Team. He also serves as Director and Coach of the Dearborn Junior Jaguars, where he mentors young athletes and fosters talent at the grassroots level.
